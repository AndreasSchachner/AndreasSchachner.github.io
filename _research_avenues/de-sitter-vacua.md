title: de Sitter vacua in string theory

description: Constructing and stress-testing candidate de Sitter vacua in string theory, and embedding them in compactifications that realise realistic particle phenomenology.
---

<div style="width: 750px;">
   <p align="justify">
    Explaining the observed accelerated expansion of the Universe within
    a UV-complete theory of quantum gravity is one of the central open
    problems in string phenomenology. Whether controlled de Sitter vacua
    exist in string theory remains an active and contested question. My
    work aims both to construct explicit candidate vacua and to subject
    them to increasingly stringent consistency checks.
   </p>
</div>
<br>

### Candidate vacua

<div style="width: 750px;">
   <p align="justify">
    In
    <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13751" target="_blank">arXiv:2406.13751</a>
    we presented explicit candidate de Sitter vacua in a concrete
    Type&nbsp;IIB compactification, assembling the required ingredients
    &mdash; fluxes, non-perturbative effects, and an uplift sector &mdash;
    within a single controlled construction. A short companion overview
    is available as
    <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00149" target="_blank">arXiv:2505.00149</a>
    (Corfu 2024 proceedings).
   </p>
</div>
<br>

### Uplifts, warping, and pedagogy

<div style="width: 750px;">
   <p align="justify">
    In
    <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17995" target="_blank">arXiv:2512.17995</a>
    we examined the role of warping in effective potentials relevant for
    F-term uplifting, clarifying under which conditions the standard
    approximations used in KKLT-type constructions remain reliable. A
    pedagogical treatment of the subject was given in the TASI 2025
    lectures,
    <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17095" target="_blank">arXiv:2512.17095</a>,
    co-authored with Liam McAllister.
   </p>
</div>
<br>

### Connecting to phenomenology

<div style="width: 750px;">
   <p align="justify">
    A recurring motivation is that a de Sitter solution is only useful
    once it coexists with realistic particle physics. This was the guiding
    question in
    <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11964" target="_blank">arXiv:2106.11964</a>,
    where we embedded a Standard-Model-like quiver gauge theory in de
    Sitter-compatible Type&nbsp;IIB compactifications.
   </p>
</div>
<br>

### Ongoing directions
